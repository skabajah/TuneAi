Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 31, 2026

info 1. Overview

TuneAi ("we", "our", "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our Chrome extension.

block 2. Data Collection

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

The extension operates entirely locally within your browser. No data is sent to any external servers, including our own.

storage 3. Data Storage

The extension uses Chrome's built-in storage API (chrome.storage.sync and chrome.storage.local) to store:

  • Your platform preferences (which AI platforms you have enabled)
  • Your custom persona prompts (if you create any)
  • Sidebar settings (open/closed state, width)

All data is stored locally on your device and never leaves your browser.

lock 4. Permissions Justification

Permission Why We Need It
storage To save your platform preferences and custom persona prompts locally
activeTab To detect the current tab and insert prompts into the chat input
scripting To inject the sidebar interface into supported AI chat pages
host_permissions To allow the sidebar and prompt insertion on supported AI platforms (DeepSeek, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Local Llama)

public 5. Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking tools.

The extension loads Google Material Icons from Google's CDN. This may transmit your IP address to Google's servers. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

share 6. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your data with anyone.

gavel 7. Your Rights

Since we collect no personal data, there is no data to access, modify, or delete. All data stored locally by the extension can be cleared at any time:

  • Chrome: Right-click the extension icon → Manage Extension → Storage → Clear
  • Or clear all Chrome extension data via Chrome's settings

update 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
